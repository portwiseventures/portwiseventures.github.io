Who We Work With

Six kinds of partner

Manufacturers

Producers of jute, leather, agricultural, food and other export products.

Product suppliers

Suppliers and aggregators with products that suit our brands.

International distributors

Distributors and retailers who want to sell our brands in their market.

Importers

Importers looking for reliable sourcing from Bangladesh.

Logistics providers

Freight, port, warehousing and inspection service providers.

Strategic business partners

Organisations with a shared interest in trade and market development.

Evaluation Process

How applications are handled

Being open to partnerships does not mean every application is accepted.

Step 1

Application review

We read every application and check it against our current needs.

Step 2

Information request

We may ask for company details, product information or documents.

Step 3

Evaluation

Suppliers are assessed with our qualification framework. Other partners are assessed for fit and reliability.

Step 4

Decision and next steps

We reply with the outcome. A positive reply does not guarantee orders or volumes.
