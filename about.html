About Portwise Ventures

A Chattogram-based parent company built to simplify and empower global trade.

Who we are

Portwise Ventures connects producers and manufacturers in emerging markets with buyers around the world. We operate as a merchant exporter: we source from verified mills and manufacturers and take responsibility for quality, documentation, communication and delivery. The company was formerly known as Crossloop.

Mission

To simplify and empower global trade by providing trusted export-import operations, strategic business consultation, and logistics solutions that connect emerging markets with global opportunity.

Vision

To become a leading cross-border trade partner known for smart logistics, reliable networks, and empowering businesses across frontiers.

Our business model

Portwise Ventures operates as a merchant exporter. We source from verified mills and manufacturers, and we take responsibility for quality, documentation, communication and delivery, so buyers deal with one trusted partner from inquiry to port.

Our business ecosystem

Four brands, one parent company. Each brand serves its own market, and all share the Portwise Ventures standards of integrity, quality and reliable delivery.

EVARA

Jute and leather products.

AGRINEST

Fresh fruits and vegetables.

PRIME CREST

Meat, fish and seafood.

LOGICWISE

Digital products and solutions.

Our international trade approach

Every order follows a clear path: you send an inquiry, we source and quote, samples and specifications are agreed, the order is confirmed with an advance payment or a confirmed letter of credit, and we manage quality checks, export documents, port handling and carrier booking. See How to Order for the full steps, or submit a sourcing requirement if you need something we do not list.
