Supplier Qualification Framework

How Portwise evaluates suppliers for your requirement.

The FrameworkOutcomesFor Suppliers

The Framework

Seven areas we look at

This is our sourcing framework. It describes how we evaluate suppliers. It does not mean that every supplier has already passed every check. We tell you in your quotation what has been checked for your requirement.

1. Business verification

Is the supplier a real, properly registered business?

  • Company registration and trade licence documents
  • Company identity, address and contact details
  • Premises information, where relevant

Confirms who you are dealing with before any commitment.

2. Product capability assessment

Can the supplier make this product to your specification?

  • Experience with the same or similar products
  • Previous samples and workmanship
  • Materials and processes available

A supplier that makes something similar is more likely to meet your specification.

3. Production capacity assessment

Can the supplier deliver your quantity on time?

  • Production equipment and workforce
  • Current workload and lead times
  • Seasonal limits, for agricultural products

Capacity and timing are confirmed for your order, not assumed.

4. Quality management evaluation

How does the supplier control quality?

  • Inspection steps during and after production
  • How defects are handled
  • Quality records, where available

Shows how consistently the supplier can repeat an approved sample.

5. Documentation review

Can the supplier support export paperwork?

  • Experience with export documents
  • Product certificates, when the supplier claims them, are requested as evidence
  • Document accuracy and speed

Accurate paperwork prevents delays at the port.

6. Communication and reliability

Is the supplier responsive and dependable?

  • Clarity and speed of replies
  • Willingness to share samples and updates
  • Delivery and trading track record, where known

Good communication prevents surprises during an order.

7. Sustainability considerations

Where relevant to the product, how are materials and workplaces managed?

  • Material sourcing and waste handling
  • Working conditions, as far as they can be reasonably checked
  • Packaging choices

Applied where relevant. See our responsible sourcing standards.

Outcomes

What happens after the evaluation

Suitable for your requirement

The supplier fits your specification and quantity. We include them in your quotation.

Needs follow-up

Some information or a sample is still needed before we can recommend the supplier.

Not suitable

The supplier does not fit this requirement. We look for another one.

Are you a manufacturer or supplier?

We are open to working with manufacturers and suppliers. Tell us about your products and capacity.
