About Evara

Evara is our jute export brand. It brings finished, value-added jute goods made from Bangladesh's renewable “golden fibre” to buyers in the UAE, UK, Canada, New Zealand, Australia, the US and Europe, sourced from verified mills in the Chattogram area.
