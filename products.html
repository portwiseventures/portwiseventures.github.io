Our Products

Natural, sustainable goods sourced from verified Bangladeshi manufacturers.

Evara

Diversified jute products

Finished and value-added jute goods, starting with custom, eco-friendly reusable shopping bags. Jute is a renewable, biodegradable natural fibre that is strong, reusable and easy to brand.

  • Custom sizes, prints and finishes
  • Sourced from verified mills in the Chattogram area
  • Samples and specifications agreed before commitment

Agrinest

Fresh vegetables and fruits

Our planned agro export lineup, built on quality grading, export-standard packaging and tight coordination between farm, port and carrier.

Primecrest

Fish, meat and seafood

Our export-oriented brand for fish, meat, seafood, crabs and dry fish. These are perishable, regulated products, so the brand is built around licensed processors, hygiene and quality checks, and reliable cold-chain handling.

  • Fresh and frozen fish and seafood
  • Crabs and meat products
  • Dry fish
