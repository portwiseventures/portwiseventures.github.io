Container Loading Planner

Estimate how many packages may fit in a shipping container.

Indicative planning tool, not a certified cargo-loading plan. It uses a simple box-in-a-grid calculation. It does not guarantee that a load is safe or feasible. Stacking limits, load distribution, pallets, packaging strength, door clearance and carrier rules all matter. Confirm with your freight forwarder.

Package and container

Use only specifications confirmed by your carrier or container supplier.

Assumptions. Typical internal dimensions and payload for standard containers (20 ft: about 5.90 x 2.35 x 2.39 m, 28,000 kg payload; 40 ft: about 12.03 x 2.35 x 2.39 m, 26,500 kg; 40 ft high cube: about 12.03 x 2.35 x 2.70 m, 26,500 kg). Door openings about 2.34 m wide and 2.28 m high (2.59 m for high cube). These are typical values and vary by container and carrier. Packages are treated as identical boxes packed in a single straight grid, with no pallets or dunnage.
