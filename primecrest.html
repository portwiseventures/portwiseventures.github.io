About Primecrest

Primecrest is our export-oriented brand for fish, meat, seafood, crabs and dry fish. These are perishable, regulated products, so the brand is built around licensed processors, hygiene and quality checks, and reliable cold-chain handling.

Product range

Fish and seafood

Fresh and frozen fish and seafood.

Crabs

Meat products

Meat sourced and handled to export standards.

Dry fish

Traditional dried fish products.

Product specifications, grades and packaging are shared on request.

Why buyers choose Primecrest

  • Licensed processors and hygiene checks
  • Cold-chain handling from source to port
  • Export documentation prepared for each shipment
  • Buyer-financed orders: sourced after advance payment or L/C

Processing & Packaging

What we confirm for each order

Processing

Processing method (fresh, frozen or dried), hygiene requirements and the processor used are confirmed for each enquiry.

Packaging and labelling

Pack size, material, label language and destination-specific labelling are agreed with you in the quotation.

Cold-chain Logistics

Temperature-controlled handling

These are perishable products. For each enquiry we confirm the required temperature, the packaging and the transport arrangement with you before an order is placed.

Transport arrangements

Refrigerated or frozen transport needs, shipping method and transit time are discussed and confirmed in your quotation.

Documentation

Export documents, plus any health or origin documents your destination requires, are discussed during quotation.
