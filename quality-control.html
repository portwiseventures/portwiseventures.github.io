Quality Control Process

Seven stages that help keep quality on track.

ProcessImportant Notes

Process

From requirement to shipment

Portwise coordinatesExternal inspection partnerBuyerSupplier

  1. Product requirement review
    PortwiseBuyer

    We review your specification, quantity, packaging and standards so everyone works from the same written requirement.

    Purpose: avoid misunderstandings later.

  2. Supplier capability assessment
    PortwiseSupplier

    We assess whether the supplier can meet the requirement, using our qualification framework.

    Purpose: choose a supplier that can do the job.

  3. Sample evaluation
    BuyerPortwiseSupplier

    A sample is made and sent to you. You evaluate it and approve it or ask for changes. See the sample evaluation guide.

    Purpose: agree the quality standard before production.

  4. Product quality inspection
    PortwiseExternal inspection partner

    Quality checks are coordinated with the supplier during or after production against the approved sample. If you need an independent report, a third-party inspection company can be arranged on request.

    Third-party inspection is carried out by an external company, not by Portwise.

  5. Packaging and labelling verification
    PortwiseSupplier

    We coordinate a check that packaging and labels match the agreed requirements. Photo evidence can be requested from the supplier.

    Purpose: prevent wrong labels or damaged packing.

  6. Pre-shipment inspection
    External inspection partnerPortwise

    Before shipping, a final inspection can be arranged with a third-party inspection company when you require one. Portwise coordinates the timing.

    Third-party inspection is an external service. Availability and cost are confirmed on request.

  7. Documentation review
    Portwise

    We review the export documents against the order before the goods ship.

    Purpose: reduce delays at the port.

Important notes

Portwise coordinates quality checks. We do not hold quality certifications on behalf of suppliers, and we do not guarantee inspection outcomes. Results depend on the standards agreed between you and the supplier and on the inspection method used.
