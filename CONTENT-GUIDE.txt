HOW TO UPDATE CONTENT (no coding needed beyond editing these files)

products.json      Products. Add an entry and a photo (p-<id>.jpg) and it appears in the marketplace, shortlist and spec sheets.
publications.json  Resource library on the Corporate Publications page.
news.json          Newsroom items.
reports.json       Market reports on the Trade Intelligence page.
capabilities.json  Factory capability profiles. Only entries with "verified": true are shown.
availability.json   Agrinest availability updates (date + text).
logicwise.json     LOGICWISE services and verified case studies (services appear in the portfolio and comparison).
site-config.json   Contact details, form recipient and analytics settings.

Only add information that has been verified.
