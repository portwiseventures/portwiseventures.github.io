Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from international buyers.

How do I buy products from Portwise Ventures?

Send us your inquiry through the quote form, WhatsApp or email. We source from verified suppliers, send a quotation, agree samples and specifications, and confirm the order. See How to Order for the five steps.

How do I request a quotation?

Use the Request a Quote form, or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us the product and brand, quantity, destination country and port, packaging needs and the date you need the goods. The more detail you give, the faster we can quote.

Can I request a product that is not listed on the website?

Yes. Tell us what you need, with specifications and quantity, and we will check what we can source for you from Bangladesh.

What are the minimum order quantities?

Minimum order quantities depend on the product and are confirmed in your quotation.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We manage export documents, port handling and carrier booking, and we offer shipping options in your quotation. Tell us your destination country and port.

What are your payment terms?

Orders are confirmed with an advance payment or a confirmed letter of credit (L/C). Details are agreed in your quotation, and orders are sourced after payment or L/C is confirmed.

Can I see product specifications?

Our catalogues list product sizes and origins, and these vary by season. For packaging and other specifications, ask us and we will confirm them in your quotation.

Can I get samples?

Samples are available on request so you can approve quality before placing an order.

Which export documents do you provide?

Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and other documents required by your destination country.

How can a manufacturer or supplier work with Portwise?

We are open to working with manufacturers and suppliers. Please contact us with your products and capacity.
