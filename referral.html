How It Works

Four simple steps

Step 1

You introduce

Tell us about a buyer, supplier or partner who may benefit from working with us.

Step 2

We make contact

We contact them only because you have confirmed they agree to hear from us.

Step 3

We assess the fit

We discuss their needs and check whether we can help.

Step 4

We let you know

If you wish, we tell you whether the introduction led to a conversation.

Eligibility

Who can refer

  • Anyone with a genuine business contact who may benefit from our services.
  • The referred person or company must know about the referral and agree to be contacted.
  • Referrals must be honest and relevant. Bulk or unsolicited lists are not accepted.
No rewards are promised. Portwise Ventures has not approved any commission, payment or benefit for referrals at this time. Referring is voluntary. If a programme of rewards is approved in future, it will be published here before it applies.
