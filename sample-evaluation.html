Sample Evaluation Guide

How to assess a product sample before you approve production.

What to CheckChecklistRequest a Sample

What to Check

Six areas to evaluate

Material and workmanship

Check that materials match what was agreed. Look at stitching, joins, finishing, colour and odour. For fresh produce, check freshness, colour, firmness and damage.

Dimensions and specifications

Measure the sample against the agreed size, weight and tolerances. Record the actual measurements.

Packaging

Check that the packing protects the product and matches the agreed style, material and quantity per pack.

Functionality

Use the product as a customer would. Test handles, closures, strength and any moving parts. For consumables, check shelf-life information.

Labelling

Check brand name, product information, origin, barcodes and any language or regulatory text required by your market.

Consistency with agreed requirements

Compare the sample with your written specification, and note every difference. Approve only what you would accept in the full order.
