PORTWISE VENTURES: WEBSITE EXPANSION, IMPLEMENTATION REPORT

IMPLEMENTED AND TESTED IN A BROWSER (no script errors)
Phase 1: Product shortlist (no account, kept in the visitor's browser for 7 days), multi-product enquiry, product comparison, specification sheet PDF (per product and multi-product), Buyer Requirement Builder (6 steps, review, email/WhatsApp fallback), Packaging & Private Label page, Buyer Matchmaker.
Phase 2: Our Sourcing Process, Supplier Qualification Framework, Quality Control Process, Sample Evaluation Guide with PDF checklist, Responsible Sourcing Standards, Factory Capability Profiles (empty framework).
Phase 3: Brand pages upgraded for EVARA, AGRINEST, PRIME CREST and LOGICWISE.
Phase 4: Partner With Us, Opportunity Board, Trade Fair Calendar (structure), Corporate Presentation (PDF profile), Email Signature Generator, Press & Media Kit, Referral Programme.
Phase 5: Global Trade Map, Export Readiness Checker with PDF checklist.
Not built, by instruction: AI Buyer Assistant.

REQUIRES AN EXTERNAL SERVICE
- All forms send through FormSubmit to info@portwiseventures.com. The first submission triggers an activation email that must be clicked once.
- No database: enquiries arrive by email only. A service such as Supabase would be needed to store them.
- Analytics are off until an ID is added to site-config.json (see ANALYTICS-SETUP.txt).
- Newsletter, currency prices and a contact map need accounts or verified data.

REQUIRES VERIFIED COMPANY INFORMATION
- PRIME CREST product specifications, LOGICWISE services and case studies.
- Factory capability profiles (capabilities.json), opportunities (opportunities.json), trade fairs (trade-fairs.json), market reports (reports.json), newsroom items (news.json).
- Material, dimension, packaging and MOQ details for EVARA; storage and grading details for AGRINEST.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- Product pages are built by script (product.html?p=ID). Search engines see them less easily than separate pages.
- Currency converter needs an internet connection to a public rate feed.
- The shortlist is stored per browser and is not shared between devices.
- India is described as planned sourcing from January 2027.
- Terms and Privacy pages should be reviewed by a lawyer.

DEPLOYMENT
Upload the files to the repository root (GitHub Pages), in batches of fewer than 100 files. Keep jspdf.umd.min.js beside the pages. Content is edited in the JSON files listed in CONTENT-GUIDE.txt.

ADVANCED EXPANSION, PHASE 1 (practical trade tools)
Built and tested: Landed Cost Calculator (user-entered duty and tax rates, PDF, reset, quotation link), Container Loading Planner (typical 20 ft, 40 ft, 40 ft high cube or user-entered container, units, visualisation, PDF, print), Time Zone Meeting Planner (Dhaka availability Sunday to Thursday 10:00-18:00, daylight saving handled by the browser, sends a request only), RFQ Template Library (10 templates, branded PDF and editable Word).
Container dimensions are typical values and must be confirmed with a carrier. Live currency rates need an internet connection. At that point, the Phase 2 intelligence features were not yet built; their current status is recorded below. Phases 3–5 are now implemented as noted below. AI assistance remains deferred at the user's request.

ADVANCED EXPANSION, PHASE 2 (trade intelligence)
Built and reviewed: Global Commodity Monitor (honest no-data state until verified price series exist); UN Comtrade Trade Statistics Explorer (key-free public preview API with bilateral exporter/importer filters, HS category/code, annual comparisons, charts, table and CSV download); Country Import Requirement Explorer (country/product/HS chapter filters, official authority references and reviewed date); Trade Agreement Explorer (searchable country/status cards, official sources and verified dates).
Data limits: UN Comtrade public preview returns at most 500 records per request and may omit records; source reporting can be incomplete or revised. Full-scale API coverage, if needed, requires a UN Comtrade account/key and a secure proxy. Commodity prices for several Portwise materials do not have a verified public price feed, so no invented values are displayed. Import rules remain general references and must be checked by HS code with the authority.
Sources reviewed 2026-11-22: UN Comtrade API documentation; European Commission GSP and EU-India agreement pages; selected government customs and regulator homepages. Some sites restrict automated retrieval.


ADVANCED EXPANSION, PHASES 3–5 (non-AI)
Built: Sustainable Materials Library and Comparison Lab with linked sources, qualitative product-specific cautions, material guide downloads and comparison CSV; Custom Product Configurator with downloadable specification and quote-form prefill; English/Arabic/French/German/Bengali selector with Arabic RTL support for shared navigation and new Phase 3–5 interface; Business Network Directory with filtering and a verified-only empty state; Smart Enquiry Centre with brand/type classification, reference number and central inbox submission; privacy-conscious event tracking for product views, downloads, form starts/submissions, trade searches and guide visits.
Limitations: Translations do not cover all legacy page content or legal/regulatory copy. Approved partner and material photographs were not provided. Enquiry routing labels the correct internal unit but uses the configured central FormSubmit inbox; secure storage and separate recipient routing need backend service and approved inboxes. Analytics remain inactive until Portwise configures its provider/account ID.
