How to Use

From template to quotation

  1. Choose the template closest to your product.
  2. Download the Word version and fill in the right-hand column. Leave a field blank if you do not know it.
  3. Email the completed form to info@portwiseventures.com, or use Fill in online to submit the same details in a form.
  4. We review your requirement and reply with questions or a quotation.
An RFQ is a request for information. It is not an order. Prices, availability and lead times are confirmed in our written quotation.
