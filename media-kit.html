Descriptions

Company and brand descriptions

Portwise Ventures

Portwise Ventures connects emerging markets with global opportunity through export-import operations, strategic business consultation and logistics solutions. It operates four specialist brands: EVARA, AGRINEST, PRIME CREST and LOGICWISE.

EVARA

Jute and leather products from Bangladesh: bags, baskets, bottle bags, gift sets and packaging.

AGRINEST

Fresh vegetables and fruits from Bangladesh for export.

PRIME CREST

An export-oriented brand for fish, meat, seafood, crabs and dry fish.

LOGICWISE

Digital products and solutions.
