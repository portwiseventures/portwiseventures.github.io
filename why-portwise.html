Company Strengths

What you get when you work with us

Integrated trade solutions

Export, import, consultation and logistics coordination under one company, so you deal with one team from inquiry to port.

International sourcing

We source from verified mills and manufacturers in Bangladesh and coordinate requirements with buyers abroad.

Supplier coordination

We communicate with suppliers on your behalf, from agreeing specifications to production updates.

Quality assurance coordination

Samples and specifications are approved before you order, and quality checks are coordinated before shipment.

Export documentation

Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and the other documents your destination requires.

Logistics coordination

Port handling and carrier booking, with shipping options included in your quotation.

Business Model

A merchant exporter you can rely on

Portwise Ventures operates as a merchant exporter. We source from verified mills and manufacturers, and we take responsibility for quality, documentation, communication and delivery. Buyers deal with one partner, and suppliers gain a route to international markets.

Operational Approach

From inquiry to delivery in five steps

Every order follows the same clear path. See How to Order for the details.

Step 1

Send your inquiry

Share the product, quantity, specifications and destination.

Step 2

Receive a quotation

We source from verified suppliers and send a clear quote with shipping options.

Step 3

Approve samples

Samples and specifications are agreed before you commit.

Step 4

Confirm the order

Advance payment or a confirmed letter of credit (L/C).

Step 5

Production and shipping

Quality checks, export documents, port handling and carrier booking, with updates.
