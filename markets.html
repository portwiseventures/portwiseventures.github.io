How an export order works

  1. Inquiry: you share product, quantity, specs and target price.
  2. Quotation: we source from verified mills and quote clearly.
  3. Sampling: samples and specs are agreed.
  4. Confirmation: order confirmed with advance payment or L/C.
  5. Production and QC: manufacturing and inspection with updates.
  6. Shipping: documents, port handling and carrier booking.
