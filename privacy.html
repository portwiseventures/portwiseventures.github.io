Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you send us.

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

Who we are

Portwise Ventures, Chattogram, Bangladesh, operates this website. Contact: info@portwiseventures.com.

What we collect

Only what you send us through our forms, WhatsApp or email: your name, company, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, country, and the product, quantity, destination and message details you give us.

How we use it

To reply to your inquiry, prepare a quotation and manage any resulting order. We do not sell your information.

How forms are sent

Our inquiry forms are delivered to our business email through the FormSubmit service. If you contact us through WhatsApp, your message is handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and third-party content

Analytics is disabled until Portwise configures a provider. If enabled, the site records page views and limited events such as product interest, resource downloads, form starts/submissions and trade-guide searches. Event data excludes form field values and contact details. Google Analytics loads only after consent; the optional Plausible configuration is cookieless. Where the Google consent banner is shown, you can change your choice using “Cookie settings” in the footer. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and handle any business relationship, and as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information you sent us by emailing us at the address above.

Business Network: buyers, suppliers and submissions

When you complete a buyer registration, supplier registration, product request, product submission or supplier response, we collect the business and contact details you enter (for example company name, registration number, address, contact person, email, phone and WhatsApp number), the product, quantity, pricing and commercial information you provide, and any documents, photographs or videos you upload.

We use this information to review and verify businesses, match buyers with suitable suppliers, manage sourcing opportunities and quotations, respond to you, and keep an internal record of our sourcing activity. We may also use it to contact you about the submission by email, phone or WhatsApp.

Privacy of what you send. Buyer and supplier information is stored privately and is available only to authorised Portwise Ventures staff. It is not published, and it is not shown to other buyers or suppliers. A supplier who responds to a sourcing opportunity cannot see other suppliers’ responses or quotations, and cannot see buyer information. We publish a product, photograph or video only after our own review and approval, and we never publish supplier or buyer identities, contact details or prices without explicit approval.

Uploaded files are stored in private storage with restricted access and are opened by staff through short-lived secure links. Photographs published in our Visual Journal are resized and stripped of location data before publication.

Service providers. To run these services we use: Supabase (database, file storage and staff sign-in), Resend (sending confirmation and notification emails) and Cloudflare Turnstile (spam protection, which checks that a form is submitted by a person). These providers process data on our behalf to deliver the service.

Activity records. We keep an internal activity history of changes to business records (for example a status change or a note), and we may keep technical information such as a short-lived anonymised network identifier for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

Retention and your rights. We keep this information for as long as needed to assess your submission and handle any resulting business relationship, and as required by law. You can ask to access, correct or delete your information by emailing us at the address above.

Changes

We may update this policy. Last updated: 22 November 2026.
