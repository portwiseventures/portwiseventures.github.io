Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you send us.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who we are

Portwise Ventures, Chattogram, Bangladesh, operates this website. Contact: info@portwiseventures.com.

What we collect

Only what you send us through our forms, WhatsApp or email: your name, company, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, country, and the product, quantity, destination and message details you give us.

How we use it

To reply to your inquiry, prepare a quotation and manage any resulting order. We do not sell your information.

How forms are sent

Our inquiry forms are delivered to our business email through the FormSubmit service. If you contact us through WhatsApp, your message is handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy.

Cookies and third-party content

We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies on this website. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads. If we add analytics or similar tools in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and handle any business relationship, and as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information you sent us by emailing us at the address above.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows when it was last changed.
